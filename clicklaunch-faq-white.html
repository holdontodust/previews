Why is my business not showing up on Google?

Usually one of a few fixable reasons. Your Google Business Profile may not be claimed or verified yet. Your business info — name, phone, hours, services — may be missing, wrong, or inconsistent across the web. Your website may not clearly say what you do and where you do it. Or competitors near you simply have stronger profiles and more reviews, so Google shows them first. And sometimes it's just newness: a new business or website can take a while to get picked up. The fix starts with finding which of these it is — that's the first thing we check.

What is local SEO?

It's how a business gets found by people nearby who are ready to hire. When someone searches “plumber near me” or opens Google Maps, local SEO is what decides which businesses show up. It covers your Google Business Profile, your reviews, your website clearly stating your services and service areas, and your business information being consistent everywhere it appears online. Regular SEO is about the whole internet — local SEO is about your own backyard, where your customers actually are.

Is SEO worth it for a small business?

For most local service businesses, yes. Your customers are already on Google searching for exactly what you do, usually at the moment they need it. Showing up right then is the most valuable visibility there is — and unlike ads, it doesn't stop the second you stop paying. The honest exception: if all your work comes from one referral source and you're already booked solid, you may not need it. If you want the phone to ring more, this is where it comes from.

How do I get my business on Google?

Start with a Google Business Profile: create or claim your listing, add your name, phone, hours, services, and service area, then verify it — Google usually checks by video or postcard. Once verified, you can appear in Maps and local search. After that, the work is keeping the profile complete, collecting reviews, and having a website that clearly says what you do, so Google trusts the listing enough to show it. That sequence is the foundation of everything we set up for clients.
