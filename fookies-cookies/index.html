Meet the baker

It started with one oven and a strong opinion.

Most cookies are built to survive a warehouse. Fookies are built to be eaten today. Every batch starts the night before, rests overnight so the flavor actually develops, and goes into the oven first thing in the morning.

We keep the batches small on purpose. When a batch sells out, it's gone, and we'd rather sell out than sell you a cookie that's been sitting around losing its soul.

That's the whole business model: bake it fresh, bake it right, and stop apologizing for it.

, The Fookies Baker
The tests

We put our cookies through it

Anybody can say "gooey." We'd rather show you.

Test 01

The Gooey Test

Pull a warm Fookie apart and the chocolate stretches with it. That's not a sauce, a filling, or a trick. That's what happens when a cookie is loaded with real chocolate and pulled out of the oven at the right minute instead of the safe one.

Test 02

The Dunk Test

Three seconds in cold milk, held together. Five seconds, still holding. A Fookie is built thick enough to dunk without disintegrating into cookie soup at the bottom of your glass.

Test 03

The Day-Two Test

If one somehow survives the night, ten seconds in the microwave brings the center right back to warm and gooey. Day two, same cookie. Try that with a store cookie and you get a warm hockey puck.
