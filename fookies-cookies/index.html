Inside Every Cookie

Simple ingredients. Nothing to hide.

Low sugar, natural, simple, delicious. Here's exactly what goes into a Fookie, and what never does.

01

Grass Fed Butter

Real butter from grass fed cows. It's where the rich, luxuriously deep flavor starts, and why our minis taste like a treat, not a compromise.

02

Pasture Raised Eggs

Eggs from hens raised on pasture, not in cages. Better eggs make a better cookie, in texture, color, and taste.

03

Unbleached Flour

Flour the natural way, never bleached with chemicals. It's a small choice that changes every single bite.

04

Low Natural Sugar

Sweet, never a sugar bomb. We keep the sugar low and natural so the chocolate and butter do the talking.

05

No Artificial Ingredients

No artificial flavors, no artificial colors, no ingredients you can't pronounce. If it isn't real, it isn't in a Fookie.

06

No Seed Oils

Most cookies are baked with cheap seed oils. Ours never are. Grass fed butter does the job, and does it better.
